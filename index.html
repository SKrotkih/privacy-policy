Emdalex Stream — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Emdalex Stream (“the app”) is an iOS application developed by Serhii Krotkih (“we”, “us”). It lets you schedule and run live streams from your iPhone to your own YouTube channel and watch your recordings. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and where it goes.

In short: the app has no servers of its own and does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens between your device and YouTube (Google), on your behalf and under your Google account.

1. Data the app uses

Your YouTube account

To create, start, stop, edit and delete live streams and to list your recordings, the app signs you in with Google and uses the YouTube Data API on your behalf. The app requests the following Google permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • youtube / youtube.force-ssl — manage your YouTube account: create and manage live broadcasts and streams, list and delete your videos.
  • openid, email, profile — your name, e-mail address and profile picture, shown in the app’s side menu so you can see which account is signed in.

The Google access token, and the basic profile information above, are stored only on your device, in the secure iOS keychain / app storage, and are removed when you sign out or delete the app. They are never sent anywhere except to Google’s own APIs.

Camera and microphone

When you start a stream, the app captures video and audio and sends them directly to YouTube’s ingestion servers (RTMP). The app does not record, store or analyse this content; the recording that appears in YouTube afterwards is created by YouTube under your account.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your current location once, when you create a stream, to pre-fill the stream description with a street address (for example “Main Street 12, Springfield”). You can edit or delete this text before saving. The address becomes part of the stream’s description on YouTube; it is not stored by the app and is not used for anything else. Location is used only while the app is open (“When In Use”), and the app works without it.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you when a scheduled stream is due to start. These are generated on the device only; no push-notification service is involved.

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not contain analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not send any data to us or to third parties other than Google/YouTube as described above. We have no access to your Google account, your streams or your location.

3. Google API Services — Limited Use

Emdalex Stream’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app’s live-streaming features that you can see in the app, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to anyone except as necessary to provide those features.

4. Third parties

The only third party that processes your data is Google (Google Sign-In, YouTube Data API, YouTube live ingestion and playback). Their handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

5. Your control

  • Sign out in the app’s side menu to remove the Google token and profile data from your device.
  • Revoke the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Camera, microphone, location and notification permissions can be changed at any time in iOS Settings → Emdalex Stream.
  • Streams and recordings can be deleted from the app or directly in YouTube Studio; deletion is performed in your YouTube account.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and requires a Google account that meets Google’s age requirements.
