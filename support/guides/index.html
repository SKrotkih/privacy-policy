Emdalex Stream — Guides

Two things you do outside the app: setting up a shared channel for your group, and taking the app's access away from your Google Account when you no longer need it.

Create a group channel Remove the app's access

1. Create a YouTube channel for your group

A group (a family, a team, a class) streams to one shared YouTube channel. Each member signs in to Emdalex Stream with their own Google account and simply picks the group channel — nobody shares a password. The channel is a YouTube Brand Account; the person who creates it is its owner and can add and remove members at any time.

Do this once, on a computer, in a web browser. It takes about ten minutes, plus a one-time wait of up to 24 hours before YouTube allows the new channel to stream.

Step 1 — Create the channel

  1. Go to youtube.com and sign in with the Google account that will own the group channel (yours, if you are the coach or the parent).
  2. Open youtube.com/channel_switcher. You will see your existing channel and a tile with a plus sign.
  3. Click Create a channel, enter the group's name (for example Smith Family or Tuesday Swim Group) and click Create. You can leave the picture and the handle as they are.

Step 2 — Enable live streaming on the channel

  1. While the new channel is selected, open studio.youtube.com.
  2. Click Create (top right) → Go live. YouTube will ask you to verify a phone number by SMS.
  3. After verification YouTube shows a countdown: live streaming becomes available on this channel within 24 hours. You can close the page — nothing else is needed. Until the countdown ends, the app cannot create streams on this channel.

Step 3 — Make sure members are managed through Google Account

YouTube has two ways to give people access to a channel. Only one of them works with apps like Emdalex Stream: Brand Account managers, added on myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts. People invited through YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions can use the channel on the YouTube website only, not from the app.
  1. Open myaccount.google.com/brandaccounts and click the group channel.
  2. If you see a Manage Permissions button under Users, go to Step 4.
  3. If there is no such button, the channel was created on the newer permissions system. Switch it back: open studio.youtube.com → Settings (bottom left) → Permissions → Opt out of permissions in YouTube Studio and confirm. Reload the Brand Accounts page — Manage Permissions is now there.

Step 4 — Invite the members

  1. On the channel's Brand Account page click Manage Permissions.
  2. Click the invite icon (a person with a plus sign), enter the member's Google e-mail address and choose the role Manager. Repeat for each member, then click Invite.
  3. Each member receives an e-mail from Google and must click Accept in it. Until they do, the channel does not appear for them in the app.

Roles. A Manager can stream, watch and delete streams — the right role for members. An Owner can also add and remove people; give it only to a second adult you trust. To remove someone later, open the same page and click the cross next to their name.

Step 5 — Sign in on the phones

  1. On each phone, open Emdalex Stream. If the app is already signed in to a personal channel, open the side menu and tap Log Out.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google and choose your own account.
  3. On the next screen, Choose your account or a brand account, tap the group channel, not your personal profile.
  4. Enter your name when the app asks — it is added to your streams so the group can tell whose stream is whose.

Everyone who signs in this way sees the same lists: what is scheduled, who is on air (the Live tab) and all recordings. Streams are unlisted by default, so only people with the link — in practice, the group — can watch them.

2. Remove Emdalex Stream's access to your Google Account

When you signed in for the first time, Google asked you to allow Emdalex Stream to manage your YouTube account. If you stop using the app — or simply want to tidy up your account — you can take that permission away. The app keeps nothing anywhere else, so this is all there is to remove.

On your iPhone

  1. Open Safari and go to myaccount.google.com/connections. Sign in if asked. (The same page is reachable from the Google or Gmail app: tap your picture → Manage your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.)
  2. In the list of connected apps, tap Emdalex Stream.
  3. Tap Delete all connections you have with Emdalex Stream (on some accounts the button reads Remove access), then Confirm.

If you use a group channel, the app appears once per Google account, not per channel — removing it disconnects both your personal channel and the group channel.

Then, in the app

  1. Open the side menu and tap Log Out. This removes the saved sign-in from the phone.
  2. To also remove the name you entered and your settings, delete the app: press and hold its icon → Remove App → Delete App.

What is not affected

Your streams and recordings stay on YouTube — they belong to your channel, not to the app. Manage or delete them in YouTube Studio at any time. If you later sign in to Emdalex Stream again, Google will simply ask for the permission once more.
